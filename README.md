## 1. Project Overview

FleetSafe Pro is an interactive fleet safety and compliance management application developed around the operational requirements of commercial vehicle fleets, transport businesses and vehicle-related workplaces.

The application brings together several important fleet management and occupational health and safety functions in one digital environment. It provides a structured way to maintain vehicle and driver records, identify hazards, assess risks, conduct inspections, record incidents, manage corrective actions and monitor compliance-related information.

The project explores how practical operational processes and safety documentation can be organised into a more accessible, connected digital system.

Rather than managing inspections, risk assessments, incident records and corrective actions as disconnected administrative tasks, FleetSafe Pro aims to bring these activities together to support better information visibility, follow-up and accountability.

## 2. Why This Project Was Developed

Fleet and workshop environments involve many interconnected responsibilities. Vehicle condition, driver fitness, maintenance, workplace hazards, inspections, incidents and compliance documentation all contribute to operational safety.

When this information is spread across separate spreadsheets, paper forms and registers, it can become difficult to maintain consistent records, identify outstanding issues and ensure that findings receive appropriate attention.

FleetSafe Pro was developed to explore a more organised approach.

The objectives were to:

- Bring important fleet and safety information together in one application.
- Simplify the recording and retrieval of operational information.
- Provide structured digital inspection and risk assessment processes.
- Improve visibility of outstanding safety issues and corrective actions.
- Connect identified risks and inspection findings with follow-up activities.
- Support more consistent record-keeping and responsibility tracking.
- Provide management with a clearer overview of fleet safety and compliance information.
- Demonstrate how practical operational knowledge can be translated into a functional digital application.

The broader objective was to demonstrate how digital workflow design can reduce administrative fragmentation and help organisations manage information more effectively.

## 4. Key Features and Modules

### Dashboard

Provides an overview of fleet safety and compliance information, including key indicators, outstanding actions, risk information and management summaries.

### Fleet Management

A central register for fleet vehicles, trailers and related assets.

Information includes:

- Vehicle and asset identification.
- Registration and vehicle details.
- Assigned drivers.
- Vehicle condition and operational status.
- Licence, insurance and Certificate of Fitness (COF) expiry information.
- Maintenance and service-related information.

### Driver Management

A central driver register for maintaining relevant driver and safety information.

Includes:

- Driver details and contact information.
- Driving licence and Professional Driving Permit (PDP) information.
- Medical and training records.
- Expiry monitoring.
- Emergency contact information.
- Driver-related safety information.

### HIRA – Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

A structured hazard identification and risk assessment module.

Features include:

- A predefined hazard library.
- Operational, vehicle, workshop and workplace hazard categories.
- Likelihood and severity scoring.
- A 5 × 5 risk assessment matrix.
- Initial and residual risk ratings.
- Risk classification.
- Hierarchy of controls.
- Assigned responsibility and review information.
- Corrective action links.

The module supports a structured approach to identifying hazards, evaluating risks and recording appropriate control measures.

### Vehicle and Workplace Inspections

A collection of inspection checklists covering different fleet and workplace activities.

Inspection areas include:

- Daily vehicle inspections.
- Trailer inspections.
- Workshop safety.
- Brake and tyre inspections.
- Load security.
- Forklift inspections.
- Recovery and towing equipment.
- Fire equipment.
- First aid equipment.

Inspection results can be recorded using Pass, Fail and Not Applicable selections, with supporting comments and electronic sign-off.

Failed inspection items can generate corrective actions for follow-up.

### Incident Management

A structured incident and near-miss recording module.

Supports the recording and investigation of:

- Vehicle accidents and damage.
- Workplace injuries.
- Near misses.
- Property damage.
- Environmental incidents.
- Other safety-related events.

Includes investigation information and root-cause analysis support, including the Five Whys approach.

### CAPA – Corrective and Preventive Actions

A central register for tracking corrective and preventive actions.

Includes:

- Action descriptions.
- Assigned responsibility.
- Target dates.
- Progress and status tracking.
- Overdue action monitoring.
- Supporting evidence and close-out information.

Corrective actions can originate from other safety activities, including risk assessments and inspections.

### Compliance Management

Provides a structured register for monitoring safety-related documentation and compliance requirements.

Includes document records, expiry monitoring and audit scheduling.

### Reports and Analytics

Provides management-level visibility of recorded fleet and safety information.

Includes summaries and reporting for areas such as:

- Fleet status.
- Risk assessments.
- Inspections.
- Incidents.
- Corrective actions.
- Compliance information.

Data export functionality supports further analysis and record-keeping.

### Administration and Settings

Provides application configuration and administrative functions, including:

- Company profile information.
- Assessor and user profile details.
- Company logo settings.
- Display themes.
- Expiry alert settings.
- Data backup and restore options.

## 5. Connected Safety Management Approach

An important aspect of FleetSafe Pro is the relationship between its different modules.

The intended management approach is to support a connected process:

1. Register the vehicle, driver, asset or workplace.
2. Identify potential hazards and operational risks.
3. Conduct inspections and record findings.
4. Record incidents and investigate contributing factors.
5. Generate and assign corrective or preventive actions.
6. Monitor responsibilities, deadlines and progress.
7. Review outstanding matters through management dashboards and reports.

For example, a failed vehicle inspection can result in a corrective action requiring investigation and follow-up. Similarly, an identified operational hazard can be recorded through HIRA and linked to appropriate control measures and actions.

This approach aims to reduce the possibility of safety findings being recorded without appropriate follow-up.

### Example Workflow

```text

             |
```

## 7. Technology and Application Structure

FleetSafe Pro is implemented as a self-contained web application.

| Technology | Application |
|---|---|
| HTML5 | Application structure |
| CSS3 | Styling and interface presentation |
| JavaScript | Application logic and interactive functionality |
| Browser Local Storage | Local data storage |
| GitHub | Source code repository |
| GitHub Pages | Application hosting and deployment |

The application is contained in a single `index.html` file, with its interface, styling and application logic included in that file.

This structure allows the application to be accessed through a web browser without requiring a traditional software installation.

## 8. Data Storage and Current Limitations

FleetSafe Pro is a portfolio-stage application and uses browser-based Local Storage.

This means:

- Information is stored locally in the browser being used.
- Data is not automatically synchronised between different computers or devices.
- Clearing browser data may result in the loss of locally stored information.
- The application does not currently use a central database.
- It does not provide production-grade user authentication or multi-user access controls.

The application includes backup and restore functionality to support local data management.

> **Important:** FleetSafe Pro is a demonstration and development project. It should not currently be used as a replacement for a professionally implemented fleet management or legally validated OH&S management system.

Safety procedures, assessment criteria, inspection checklists and compliance requirements should be reviewed and approved by suitably qualified professionals before operational use.
